MODEL & LIMITATIONS

A testable hypothesis,
not a promised advantage.

This simulator compares a conventional body and an adaptive body under the same initial mass, speed and environment. The moving parts and numerical results are generated by MuJoCo, running directly in your browser.

What is modeled

Rigid shell segments connected by springs, dampers and neighboring mechanical links; passive crush members; an engine on a restrained track; proximity sensing; bounded rule-based control; plastic link deformation; resource-limited repair of minor coating defects.

What the colors mean

Damage and contact loads are calculated per mechanical element. They are not continuum stress fields. Damage is a reduced plastic-link/coating proxy. The passenger cell is rigid; its acceleration is not an injury prediction.

Established · Experimental · Hypothetical

Mass–spring–damper mechanics and passive crush structures have established engineering analogues. The coordinated adaptive body and autonomous repair in this model are hypothetical. Parameters are illustrative and not calibrated to a production car.

Scope

There is no FEM, tire-road model, airbag, occupant biomechanics, trained AI, lifetime simulation or live cloud evolution in this browser version. The engine and its constraints are the same MuJoCo version as the Python desktop prototype. Models are generated from that Python source; the browser contains a port of the bounded controller, repair and damage rules.

Your experiments run locally in the browser. Settings may be remembered on your device; no personal profile is uploaded. JSON exports contain the exact conditions and trajectories.
